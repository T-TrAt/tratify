Material Design 3 components vendored from floor/material 3.2.4

Copyright (c) 2025 floor
License: MIT. The full license is preserved in vendor/material/LICENSE and
vendor/material/generated/LICENSE, alongside the distributed runtime.
Source: https://github.com/floor/material
Revision: c910072f3fc5b660276731d8a935d7186f288364

The component factories, web elements, Vue adapters and Sass styles are
preserved in vendor/material/src. Apptify's build resolves the shadow-style
imports locally and restricts the document-level theme tokens to containers
with class "apptify-material". The original Apple components remain separate.
Generated shadow styles also restore native closed-dialog display rules for
drawer, bottom-sheet and side-sheet surfaces.
Local menu positioning patches follow opener layout changes and correct
edge alignment. The carousel direction observer ignores unrelated dir mutations
to preserve continuous scrolling. Modal drawers, bottom sheets and side sheets
retain display and their top-layer overlay until the reverse slide-out transition
completes. Timepicker and modal datepicker styles follow the dialog's surface
motion, staggered entry timing and backdrop fades. Picker contents stay with the
surface throughout exit; cancelling a time selection preserves its painted DOM
until the next open. Calendar view updates do not replay entry effects. Patched
source paths are recorded in vendor/material/SOURCE.json.
